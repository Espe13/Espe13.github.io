Code & Data

I build differentiable, GPU-native tools for stellar-population inference and use them to produce survey-scale data products. The code is open source under the MIT licence; contributions, issues and feature requests are welcome.

CERIDWEN · github.com/Espe13/ceridwen

A GPU-native, end-to-end differentiable SED-fitting framework written in JAX. CERIDWEN (Comprehensive SED Estimation Routine Involving Data-driven Weight calculatioNs) covers the full forward model, from simple stellar populations through nebular emission and dust to the projection onto the observed frame, so that posteriors over stellar mass, star-formation history, chemical enrichment, dust and redshift can be sampled on the GPU.

Production inference uses a GPU nested slice sampler, with Hamiltonian Monte Carlo and variational inference available for the same model. It supports non-parametric star-formation histories with of order one hundred age bins and jointly sampled chemical enrichment, and is a median of 134 times faster than CPU Prospector on high-redshift galaxies.

JADES fit explorer · interactive catalogue

Within the JADES collaboration I lead the construction of a value-added catalogue of inferred physical properties (stellar masses, star-formation histories, dust, metallicities and ionising properties) for the galaxies with both NIRCam photometry and NIRSpec spectroscopy, fitted jointly with CERIDWEN. The fit explorer shows these fits and their posteriors: a searchable catalogue table with a page per galaxy, and the SED manifold, which displays scaling relations as rotating projections of the fitted parameter space, with filters on redshift and spectroscopic quality.

sedpy_jax · github.com/Espe13/sedpy_jax

A JAX reimplementation of sedpy (B. D. Johnson) for GPU-ready, differentiable filter handling and broadband photometric projection: transmission curves, AB and Vega magnitudes, flux-conserving rebinning, air–vacuum conversion, and effective, pivot and zero-point wavelengths, vectorised over many filters at once.
